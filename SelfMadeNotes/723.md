## 2

**Definition:**
Let $U$ be a unitary operator that acts on basis vectors in the following way

For each basis vector $\ket{b}$ and $\ket{i}$ we have that 

$$U_x(\ket{i}\otimes\ket{b}) = (1 -x_i )\ket{i}\ket{1-b} + x_i\ket{i}\ket{b}$$

**Claim:** Let $T= Q(f)$ be the query complexity of $f$. We claim $2T \geq deg(f)$

**Proof:** Suppose $U_x$ is applied $T$ times. Then by definition of $U_x$, the vector $\ket{\psi_T} = U^T_x\ket{\psi_0}$ is a linear combination with coeficients that are polynomials in $x$. Evidently the degree is $T-1$. Hence 

$$Deg (f) = T - 1 \implies Deg (f) \leq T$$
